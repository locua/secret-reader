Reader

An EPUB reader that looks and works like a word processor.

Reader opens .epub books in your browser and lays each chapter out as printed pages: a ribbon of formatting controls at the top, a navigation pane with the book’s contents at the side, and a status bar along the bottom.

Open Reader

Your books stay on your device

Books you open are stored in this browser only (IndexedDB) together with your reading position. Nothing is uploaded and there is no server: Reader is a set of static files. Clearing your browser’s site data removes the stored books.

What it does

  • Change the font, size, line spacing, alignment and paragraph style; the pages reflow to match.
  • Choose a page size (Letter, A4, A5, 6″ × 9″), margins and page colour (white, sepia or dark).
  • Jump to any chapter or section from the Navigation pane.
  • Search the whole book from the search box in the title bar or the Navigation pane. Matches are highlighted on the page.
  • Zoom to 100%, the page width or a whole page.
  • Reopen recent books from File; each one opens where you left off.

The Insert and References tabs are for show: their menus do nothing.
